## 1. What "passing" has to mean, and the honest ceiling on 200k
- An organisation that publishes only `info@` has `info@` as its invoicing channel; that is the owner's own rule of 2026-10-28 (`ap_email_type=general`).
  So "passing" = **a complete invoice (bill-to legal name + address, vendor legal name + GST/HST + remit-to, tax, currency) to a referee-confirmed
  channel**, i.e. pay-on-sight `yes`, `yes — no PO rule` or `routed`. Today 38 %. The 85 % target means ~54k more complete rows at today's size, or
  170k of 200k.
- What the 39,626 incomplete rows lack (measured): **bill-to legal name / address 33,136** (17,902 have an address but no published legal name,
  11,383 neither, 3,845 a name but no address), tax treatment 13,619 (follows the address: province or country unknown), vendor GST 10,503
  (ceiling for ~all: no public registry; ask the vendor), currency 3,007 (follows the country), vendor address 1,547. 22,394 rows lack exactly one
  element. **Bill-to is the lever**: 26,180 of those buyers' sites answer; 24,670 are `.ca`.
- 200k rows: the public evidence universe is a pyramid. Public bodies with contract records (strong rows) number in the low tens of thousands
  worldwide for Canadian vendors; company buyers proven from vendors' own client lists, case studies and filings (P13-type) run to the hundreds of
  thousands but at general-inbox grade. 200k is reachable only by adding both layers; 200k at 85 % complete depends on how far registries and
  extraction can close the bill-to gap for companies. **I will not promise 200k before the week-1 pilots measure the yield per source class**; the
  projection to Jan 10 goes in Sunday's report with the method shown.

**T3 Inference layer — the owner's "Monday-to-Friday" rule, with numbers.** For organisation-type policies, peer-group base rates from our own

  not a value — the invoice states the vendor's terms. Identity fields get convergence scores (how many independent sources agree); relationships
  (P13) get a signal-count confidence. Every inferred value in its own `*_inferred` column with p, n and sources; never counted as published.
**T4 Feedback — the Gold pilot.** 833 rows are pay-on-sight today. If the owner lets the invoice builder start on Gold now, every payment and every
  bounce becomes ground truth that calibrates T3 and T2. Two months of inference with zero real feedback is the main risk of waiting.
