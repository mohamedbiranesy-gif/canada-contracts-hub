# us_universities — UC, CSU, SUNY, Texas A&M system procurement: checked, rejected

UCOP procurement-services pages are policy/supplier-registration content (no award register); calstate.edu returns 403 to non-browser clients; SUNY awards appear only through NYS OSC Open Book (interactive, errors on scripted queries) and the SUCF contracts dataset (cfjm-ii27, all vendors Country=USA); Texas A&M system contracts ≥ $50k are reported to the Texas LBB database (covered in `us_state_texas_lbb`). Not pulled separately.
