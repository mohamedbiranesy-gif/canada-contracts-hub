## How "Canadian vendor" was determined (names never used)
1. `parties[role=supplier].address.country == "CA"` or `address.countryName` contains "Canada" (127 records; both forms occur because older TED-schema notices only carry `countryName`).
2. If the source states another country but `locality/region/postalCode` are unmistakably Canadian (Canadian postal code `A1A 1A1` or "Canada" in locality), the party is included **flagged** `vendor.country_conflict = true` with the conflict spelled out in `vendor.country_basis` (4 records: Aquatic Informatics "Vancouver BC V6E 4M3" with countryName United Kingdom; Colt Canada "Kitchener N2C 1J3" with United States; Rheinmetall Canada "Quebec J3B 8E9" with United Kingdom; Hootsuite "Vancouver, BC, Canada" with GB). Two further records are flagged the other way: country field says Canada but the street address looks like a US address (Twist Bioscience, South San Francisco CA 94080) — included because the source's country field says Canada, flagged for review.
3. Rejected (12 releases): UK/French addresses whose street contains "Canada" (One/15/20/25 Canada Square, Canada Farm Road, Canada Works, Avenue du Canada, Trans-Canada Highway in a UK Ltd's address).

## How "ongoing" was determined
Period taken from the first available of `contracts[].period`, `awards[].contractPeriod`, `tender.lots[relatedLot].contractPeriod`, `tender.contractPeriod`; for dynamic-market admissions `tender.techniques.frameworkAgreement.period`. Anchor = start, else `contracts[].dateSigned`, else award date.
- `ongoing` (62): anchor ≤ 2026-10-25 ≤ explicit end date.
- `ongoing_inferred` (18): no end date but `contractPeriod.durationInDays` present (lot or contract) → anchor + duration ≥ 2026-10-25; `status_basis` shows the arithmetic.
- `end_date_unknown` (53): award/contract published (UK4 transparency / UK7 contract-details / TED F03 notices) with no period or duration in the data; signed/award date kept in `status_basis`.
- Excluded: explicit end < today (58), inferred end < today (20), start after today (5), terminated/cancelled contracts (2).

## Record construction and de-duplication
- One record per **(contracting process, Canadian supplier, distinct contract)**. FTS compiled releases contain many near-duplicates: the same contract appears as award `N` in a UK5 transparency notice and again in the UK7 contract-details notice, or the identical award set is re-published under three notice numbers (NHS Blood and Transplant 008519/008520/008521-2025). Awards for the same supplier with the same lot(s) and the same value are collapsed into one record; the collapsed award/contract ids are kept in `contract.identifiers.collapsed_duplicate_award_ids/_contract_ids` (140 awards collapsed; 7 duplicate supplier parties inside one award skipped). Different lots with different values stay separate records (e.g. Explorance lots 1 and 2; Hootsuite lots 2b and 3).
- **Dynamic markets**: CCS RM6370 "Space Technology Solutions" (ocid ocds-h6vhtk-0510f8) lists Bornea Dynamics Limited (Ottawa) on 117 lot-admission awards with no values or dates; emitted as **one** record (`record_id` ends in the supplier party id), `instrument_type = dynamic market / dynamic purchasing system admission`, status `ongoing` from the framework period 2025-07-31 → 2031-07-30, all lots listed in `contract.identifiers.all_related_lots_for_supplier`.
- `record_id` = `uk_find_a_tender:<ocid>:<award id>` (award ids are only unique within an ocid); for multi-supplier awards (framework lots such as G-Cloud 14 with 2,744 suppliers on one lot award) the Canadian supplier's party id is appended (`…:<award id>:<GB-PPON-…>`), and for dynamic-market admissions it is `uk_find_a_tender:<ocid>:<supplier party id>`. `record_url` = `https://www.find-tender.service.gov.uk/Notice/<nnnnnn-yyyy>` taken from the award id prefix or the award/contract `documents[].url`; verified https://www.find-tender.service.gov.uk/Notice/036667-2022 → "V.2.3 Name and address of the contractor: Aquatic Informatics, 1111 West Georgia St, Vancouver, BC V6E 4M3, Country United Kingdom" (the flagged conflict case), and 047989-2026, 030786-2025 resolve. All 136 records have a `record_url`.
- Buyer: `parties[role=buyer]` with PPON (`GB-PPON-…`) / FTS party id, `details.classifications` (UK_CA_TYPE / TED_CA_TYPE) → `buyer.type`. Vendor identifiers: PPON (41), Companies House (3), FTS party id (all). CPV main + additional (tender and award items). Value: `contracts[].value` else `awards[].value` with currency (GBP 107, USD 10, EUR 4, CAD 1, none 11); framework/multi-supplier values carry `value_detail.value_note` (total across all suppliers, e.g. G-Cloud 14 £14 bn lots). Full compiled release in `raw.compiled_release`.

## Known gaps / limits
- FTS covers UK above-threshold notices from 1 Jan 2021 only (earlier UK awards are on TED; below-threshold English awards are on Contracts Finder — see that note). Scottish/Welsh portals also publish UK4/UK7 notices to FTS since Feb 2025, so some overlap with PCS/Sell2Wales is possible.
- 53 records have no end date: TED-era (2021–2024) F03 contract award notices never carried a contract period, and UK4/UK7 notices frequently omit it. Framework/DPS "awards" have no per-supplier call-off values.
- Multi-notice processes: compiled releases merge all notices of an ocid; where a later UK7 notice re-stated an earlier award with a different value or lot, both records are kept (documented above), so a few processes contribute 2 records that may be the same real-world contract (RTDS £685,000 vs £681,050; Gastops £103,000 vs £159,546.69 are different purchases per their titles).
- Bulk snapshot 2026-10-24 11:35 UTC + live API for 2026-10-23..25: no gap to the reference date.
