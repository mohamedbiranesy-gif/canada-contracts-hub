## How "Canadian vendor" was determined
From the RFx Outcome awarded-supplier entry's address: last comma-separated token (the country, upper case) == `CANADA`; fallback: address text contains "Canada" (ignoring UK "Canada Square"-type street names), or Canadian postal-code pattern + province. Names are never used (e.g. "Neo Technology Ltd - 1, Canada Square, London, E14 5AA, UNITED KINGDOM" is correctly rejected). About 17% of awarded entries carry no country token (supplier name only, e.g. "This tender has been completed. Further information: …" lists) — those cannot be classified and are a known gap. Outcome text of the form "No Winner … contract awarded to X" (free text, no address) is also unclassifiable.

## How "ongoing" was determined
GETS publishes **no contract start/end dates or values**. Rules applied:
- `ongoing_inferred` only when the notice title/overview states an explicit term ("term of three years", "5 year contract", …) and award date + term ≥ 2026-10-25; the arithmetic is in `status_basis`.
- otherwise `end_date_unknown` (award published; award date ≤ 2026-10-25) — kept and flagged per the schema.
- Excluded: award date after the reference date; inferred term already elapsed.

## Record construction
- `record_id` = `nz_gets:<RFx ID>` (suffix with supplier name if one RFx awards several Canadian suppliers). `record_url` = the detail page URL exactly as linked from the search results (agency code path segment + `?id=`), e.g. https://www.gets.govt.nz/NZIAT/ExternalTenderDetails.htm?id=34346581 (verified: page shows "RFx Outcome — This tender has been awarded to: Resonance Alliance Holding Limited - CANADA, Award Date: Thursday, 21 May 2026").
- Buyer = Organisation from the results row (also in `<title>`), agency code from the URL, Agency Address / Contact / Department fields. Vendor = parsed name + address parts (`line1`, `city`, `postal_code`, `country`). Commodity = UNSPSC categories (code + title) from the Categories list. Dates: `published` = Open Date, `signed` = Award Date, `amended_last` = "Date RFx was completed/updated". `raw` = all parsed detail fields, the list row, the verbatim awarded-supplier entry and the path of the saved HTML.

## Known gaps / limits
- No values, no contract periods in GETS notices → almost all records are `end_date_unknown`; a term is only inferable when the notice text states one.
- GETS is not a complete contract register: agencies must publish award notices for open-market tenders ≥ NZD 100k (Government Procurement Rules), but many award outcomes are entered as free text without supplier address, and closed/direct-source contracts never appear. Collaborative/AoG contracts and most council contracts are absent.
- Supplier country is as typed by the agency (e.g. "Resonance Alliance Holding Limited - CANADA" with no street address).
- The MBIE open-data CSVs (which include Supplier NZBN and probably supplier country) could not be downloaded from this environment because of the Incapsula bot challenge; re-run from a browser session if full coverage of 2014–2026 award notices with supplier identifiers is needed.
