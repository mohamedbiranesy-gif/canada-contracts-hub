## Ongoing (no explicit end date → `ongoing_inferred`, "no closure record" convention)
Intents carry award date and expected start but no end date. The Affidavit of Wages Paid is the closure record each contractor must file when its work on the project is complete. Rule per intent: affidavit exists for the intent → work completed (all 980 such intents have project completion dates before 2026-10-25 → excluded); no affidavit, intent filed (application_received_date) on/after 2023-10-25 and expected start ≤ 2026-10-25 → `ongoing_inferred` (29); no affidavit but intent filed before 2023-10-25 → excluded as stale (60); expected start after the reference date → excluded (1: FieldTurf / City of Federal Way, start 2026-11-04). The status_basis of each record quotes the intent filing date, expected start, award date and the absence of an affidavit as of retrieval.

## Record construction
One record per intent_id. Buyer = `agencyname` (awarding agency, as named: school districts, cities, counties, ports, PUDs, Sound Transit, WA Dept of Enterprise Services, universities), with agency address and phone. `instrument_type` = "public works contract (prime)" when intent_id = primeintent, otherwise "public works subcontract (hired by <hiringcompanyname>)" with the prime in `contract.related.prime_contractor`. `value` = `cntrct_amt` (the filing company's own contract amount); `value_detail.prime_contract_amount` = `amount` (prime contract total). Identifiers: intent_id, project_id, contract_number, prime intent, prime registration/UBI; vendor identifiers: WA UBI and L&I contractor registration. Contact persons' names/e-mails stay only in `raw` (not surfaced in the normalized layer). `record_url` = null: the L&I PWIA intent/affidavit search (secure.lni.wa.gov/pwia) requires a My L&I login (checked 2026-10-26).

## Gaps / caveats
- Inference only: an intent without an affidavit may also be a project that was cancelled or whose affidavit is late; limited to intents filed within 3 years.
- Postal codes are truncated by the publisher; addresses kept verbatim in `raw.intent`.
- Only Washington public works (prevailing-wage) contracts; goods/services contracts are not in these datasets.
